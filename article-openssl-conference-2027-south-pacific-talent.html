South Pacific tech talent: aim for the OpenSSL Conference in Prague in 2027

By Sangeeta Devi Deo · 19 November 2026 · 2 minute read

In 2026, the OpenSSL Foundation awarded conference scholarships to people from around the world so they could attend the OpenSSL Conference in Prague.

I would love to see someone from Fiji or the wider South Pacific among the 2027 recipients.

What is the OpenSSL Conference?

OpenSSL is the open-source software behind much of the encryption that keeps the internet secure. The OpenSSL Conference brings together the developers, security engineers, researchers, policymakers and community contributors who build and rely on it.

CyberCat took part in 2026, with a talk in the Community Track and a Wellness Corner on the conference floor. Read what OpenSSL Prague taught us.

2027: Proof of Human, Prague, September

The 2027 conference, Proof of Human, will be held in Prague in September. Keep an eye on it if you are:

  • a university student in computing, cybersecurity or mathematics
  • a researcher
  • an independent developer
  • an open-source contributor

Scholarship details for 2027 are yet to be announced. We will update this page when official information is available. Conference details are on the OpenSSL Conference website.

Why Pacific voices matter

The technology that protects global communications is used in the Pacific every day, by banks, governments, businesses and families. The people building it should hear from the region too.
