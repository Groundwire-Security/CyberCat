Privacy Policy

How CyberCat collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

This website, cybercat.au, is published by Phantasma Pty Ltd (ABN 86 142 781 271). CyberCat is a Groundwire Security initiative. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to CyberCat and Phantasma Pty Ltd.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We apply the same standards to visitors and clients in Fiji and elsewhere.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect:

  • your name and email address (required)
  • your company and phone number (optional)
  • the details you include in your enquiry.

We also collect the information you send us by email or provide when you work with us.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our website host may record basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the website secure and running.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • respond to your enquiry
  • plan and deliver workshops, experiences and other services you request
  • keep records of our communication with you.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing unless you agree to it.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with service providers that help us run this website and respond to you:

  • Formspree delivers enquiry form submissions to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we send and receive.

These providers may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We do not otherwise disclose your personal information unless you agree or the law requires it.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. The website is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection. We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete it.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, using the enquiry form at the bottom of this page. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we handle your personal information, contact us through the enquiry form. We will respond and work with you to resolve it.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
