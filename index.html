Our mission

Protect the human in a digital world.

Technology should make life and work easier, not harder. We teach practical skills, help people recognise online risks and offer short experiences that make space to pause.

Our workshops and experiences are designed for real people, not technical experts. From a small business learning new tools to someone taking a quiet moment before a spa treatment, our focus is the person using the technology.

Why the pause matters

Pressure can catch anyone out.

Scams, rushed deals and tense moments all lean on the same four pressures. Spotting them is the first step to a better decision, online or off.

TrustIt looks like someone you know.
FearSomething bad will happen if you don’t act.
UrgencyYou have to act right now.
RewardSomething too good to miss.
The PAWS method

What to do when something feels wrong.

Four simple steps for any moment of pressure: an urgent message, a contract to sign, a hiring decision or a difficult conversation.

P

Pause

Stop before you click, sign, pay or react.

A

Assess

Look for pressure, unusual requests and warning signs.

W

Walk away

Don't let anyone rush your decision.

S

Separately verify

Check with a trusted person or channel before you act.

FAQs

Common questions about CyberCat.

What does CyberCat do?

CyberCat helps people pause. We bring 90-second digital detox and wellness experiences to conferences, events, workplaces, spas and schools worldwide, and run hands-on digital skills and online safety workshops across Australia and the South Pacific.

What is the PAWS method?

PAWS is a four-step habit for any moment of pressure, from an urgent message to a contract, a hiring decision or a difficult conversation: Pause, Assess, Walk away, and Separately verify through a trusted person or channel.

What is the 90-Second Digital Wellness Reset?

A short guided audio experience, usually around 90 seconds. Participants put on headphones, take a short break and follow a message designed for their setting. No virtual reality headset or special technology is needed.

Who are CyberCat workshops for?

Small businesses, exporters, trade and development organisations, women-led business networks, chambers of commerce, educators and community organisations. Workshops suit people with mixed levels of digital experience.

Where does CyberCat work?

From our bases in Spring Hill, Queensland, and Nadi, Fiji, we run workshops across Australia and the South Pacific and bring Wellness Corners and conference activations to events internationally. In 2026 CyberCat delivered sessions in Tonga, Fiji and Prague.

Does CyberCat provide cybersecurity assessments or compliance support?

Security assessments, compliance support, risk management workshops and corporate security training are available through Groundwire Security. CyberCat is a Groundwire Security initiative.
